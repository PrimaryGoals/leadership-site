Other Tools

  • UpViral - This is like an instant affiliate program.  If you have a webinar, product, or service that people have to sign up for, you typically give a "thank you" page at the end.  UpViral extends this by allowing you to offer rewards or acknowledgment for your subscribers to share.  So if somebody signs up ("Susan," for example), then Susan gets a custom link to share.  Then anybody that follows Susan's shared link is automatically credited back to Susan, allowing you to reward her however you choose.
  • VisualPing.io (FREE) - lets you monitor any page on the web.  When content on that page changes, you get an email.  It's good if you are watching your competitors, or perhaps for a sale, or even a job posting.  You can also do a search on twitter, then monitor that search page to get notified of all tweets in your search.
