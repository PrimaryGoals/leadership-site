Coaching

What is Coaching?

“Coaching is partnering with clients in a thought-provoking and creative process that inspires them to maximize their personal and professional potential.” - International Coaching Federation
"It’s a relationship built on trust using tools and structures to let go of habits and default behaviors that delay the achievement of Your Primary Goals" - Ashley Guberman

Why I Coach

I coach people and organizations as a reflection of my fundamental belief that we are all capable of doing, creating, living, and being far more than we are in any moment.  I coach because at my very core, I stand for positive transformation in the lives of people and organizations.  I stand for bringing out the greatness of everybody that I work with.  Yes, everybody.  Because in a world where everybody is living and being who they are when they are at their very best, absolutely everything is possible.

Who I Work With

  • People who are committed to a fundamental change in their lives or the life of their company.
  • People looking for support to achieve something challenging and significant to their future.
  • Anybody looking for a bit of altitude and perspective on their own goals, for the purpose of charting a better path forward.
  • Anybody who's dreams have not yet manifested in reality, and who is ready to start taking positive steps toward making that dream real.  I mean really real - as in, "I'm ready.  Let's make this happen.  Starting now."

If you see yourself on this circle, then Contact Primary Goals and we'll put structures in place to move you forward on what's important to you.
