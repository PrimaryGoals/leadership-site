Signature Competence

  • On a sheet of paper, sign your name, using your dominant hand.
  • Now sign your name using you non-dominant hand.

Discussion

  • What are the implications of the difference?
  • You already have full knowledge of how to form the letters.
  • You have full knowledge of what you are trying to do.
  • Think about how this relates back to the  Learning Curve and Conscious Incompetence.
