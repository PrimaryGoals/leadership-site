Laughing Game

Pam Johnson led this one.
She started by demonstrating a very high, nasal laugh, which we then all imitated.

Subsequently, she demonstrated a laugh that came from the head, chest, and belly.
After each one, the group would engage in a brief laugh of their own from the target region.

Finally, she demonstrated a laugh that engaged the entire body, from top to bottom, and we repeated.

We used it to bring a bit of energy back into the group.
It could serve equally well as in ice-breaker type activity
