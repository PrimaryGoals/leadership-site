Stages of Change Commitment - Contact

Whether the initial contact with the change effort is in the form of meetings, speeches, or memos, awareness is not guaranteed.

Reasons that awareness might not be achieved:

  • The change might not be though to effect the target, in which case it will be ignored.
  • The memo/E-mail  might not even be read.
  • The contact might come from someone the target does not think important
    (a junior member of another department, for example)

Efforts to increase awareness

  • Taylor the message to the audience.
  • Check for awareness.
