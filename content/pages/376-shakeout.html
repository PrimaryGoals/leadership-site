(Presented by Megan Kennedy in Module 5, day 4, just after Market Place) Everybody", "image": "", "slug": "shakeout", "path": "/teams/activities/shakeout/", "date": "2011-09-14", "modified": "2021-07-27 11:37:02", "published_gmt": "2011-09-14T10:15:24+00:00", "modified_gmt": "2021-07-27T11:37:02+00:00", "type": "page"} --- A quick physical activity to shake out whatever was (or was not) going on. (Presented by Megan Kennedy in Module 5, day 4, just after Market Place)   Everybody stands in a large circle. Presenter indicates we all shake out our right hand above our head 8 times, then our left hand, then our right foot, then our left foot. Then repeat, only for 4 times, then again for 2 times, then finally for 1 time. The group counts 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 for each limb in unison. The entire thing takes maybe 3 minutes to present and do. It's more quick, energizing and fun than it sounds.
