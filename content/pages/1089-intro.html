  • What would happen if we increased the emotional quotient (EQ) or interpersonal skills of some of the worlds brightest and most talented individuals working on technology today?

  • What would happen if the way we work together were seen as an integral part of what we actually produced?

  • What would change if the reason why we embarked on technical ventures was as important as what we hoped to gain from them?

  • What would happen if we started to produce a culture of more Collaborative Geeks?
