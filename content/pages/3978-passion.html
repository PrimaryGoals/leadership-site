Why I Coach

I coach people and organizations as a reflection of my fundamental belief that we are all capable of doing, creating, living, and being far more than we are in any moment.  I coach because at my very core, I stand for positive transformation in the lives of people and organizations.  I stand for bringing out the greatness of everybody that I work with.  Yes, everybody.  Because in a world where everybody is living and being who they are when they are at their very best, absolutely everything is possible.  I work with my clients to help them focus on their purpose, and to move towards greater alignment with what that purpose means to them.  

Living From Purpose and Passion at Your Highest Potential

So often, we go through life without really thinking about what matters, what is important to us, why we do the things we do, or what is really possible.  In order to live a life of possibility, I believe that it is essential to look deeply and live from a sense of purpose and with conviction. People can live lives of passion, purpose, and potential.  And for organizations, the same is true by operating from mission (purpose), vision (potential) and alignment with people's beliefs and values (passion).   I believe that these factors are essential to both personal and organizational health.  If this idea resonates with you or your organization, then contact Primary Goals and let's discuss how I might be of service. “Things which matter most must never be at the mercy of things that matter least.” ~ Goethe
