Vent (Express)Carve out the time to vent -- don't be rushed.Ask permission to vent first, rather than simply dumping a load on the other.Venting includes Listening and checking the other for impact (V-L-O-M-P)
OwningHere’s my contribution to the mess...
MoccasinsThis is what I think/feel it’s like to be in your shoes.
PlanWhat’s the plan going forward; how do we do different or not repeat

note: the V-O-M part is fluid, and may cycle multiple times until Planning comes naturally.
