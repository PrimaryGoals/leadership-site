Reasons that awareness might", "image": "/Diagrams/images/StagesOfChangeCommitment_contact.gif", "slug": "contact", "path": "/teams/books/speed-of-change/stages/contact/", "date": "2011-09-27", "modified": "2021-07-28 03:42:55", "published_gmt": "2011-09-27T04:57:18+00:00", "modified_gmt": "2021-07-28T03:42:55+00:00", "type": "page"} --- Whether the initial contact with the change effort is in the form of meetings, speeches, or memos, awareness is not guaranteed.

Reasons that awareness might not be achieved:

  • The change might not be though to effect the target, in which case it will be ignored.
  • The memo/E-mail  might not even be read.
  • The contact might come from someone the target does not think important (a junior member of another department, for example)

Efforts to increase awareness

  • Taylor the message to the audience.
  • Check for awareness.
